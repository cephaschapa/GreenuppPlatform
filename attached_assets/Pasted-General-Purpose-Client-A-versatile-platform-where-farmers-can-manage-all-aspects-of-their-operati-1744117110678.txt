General Purpose Client: 
A versatile platform where farmers can manage all aspects of their operations, from monitoring crop health to accessing market prices.

1.	Crop Disease and diagnosing: AI-powered tools that use image recognition and sensor data to identify and diagnose crop diseases early, providing treatment recommendations.

2.	Crop Tracing: Blockchain-based system to trace the journey of crops from farm to table, ensuring transparency and trust in the supply chain. 

3.	Store / Market place: An online marketplace where farmers can buy and sell agricultural products, equipment, and supplies, connecting them directly to buyers.

4.	Weather Service: Real-time weather forecasting tailored to specific farm locations, helping farmers plan activities and mitigate risks related to weather conditions.

5.	Social Feed and Interaction: A social platform for farmers to share experiences, ask for advice, and collaborate on best practices, fostering a community of knowledge sharing.

6.	AI Assistance:  AI-driven insights and recommendations for improving crop yields, pest management, and resource optimization based on data analysis.

7.	Chat Service: Integrated chat service for instant communication with experts, support teams, and other farmers, facilitating quick problem resolution and information exchange.

8.	Store locator: A tool to find nearby stores that sell farming supplies, equipment, and services, helping farmers access what they need efficiently. 

9.	Payment Service: Secure, integrated payment solutions for transactions within the marketplace, ensuring smooth financial operations for farmers.

10.	Delivery Service: Logistics and delivery management for transporting agricultural products, ensuring timely and safe delivery from farms to consumers or markets.

11.	Notifications: Automated alerts and notifications for important events such as weather changes, market price shifts, pest outbreaks, and more, keeping farmers informed in real-time.



Greenupp 2.0 Features and Planning (Phase 2)

Specialized Farmer Client
A tailored interface designed specifically for farmers, focusing on ease of use and access to critical farming tools and data.
1.	Real time data capture:  Utilizing IoT devices and sensors to continuously collect data on soil health, weather conditions, crop growth, and other key metrics, providing farmers with actionable insights.

2.	Field mapping: Advanced GPS and satellite imaging technology to create detailed maps of farm fields, enabling precision farming techniques and efficient resource allocation.

3.	Drone plant monitoring: Deployment of drones equipped with cameras and sensors to monitor crop health, identify issues such as pests or diseases, and assess plant growth, all in real-time.

4.	ERP system: A comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system to manage all aspects of farm operations, including inventory, finances, labor, and supply chain management, ensuring efficient and streamlined processes.

5.	Crop management:  Tools for planning and managing crop cycles, including planting schedules, irrigation needs, fertilization plans, and harvest timelines, optimizing yield and productivity.

6.	Market price: Real-time tracking and analysis of market prices for various crops, helping farmers make informed decisions about selling their produce at the most profitable times.

7.	Training and support: Access to educational resources, tutorials, and expert consultations to help farmers improve their skills, adopt new technologies, and stay updated on best practices in agriculture.

8.	Sustainable practices and guides: Comprehensive guides and resources on implementing sustainable farming practices, reducing environmental impact, and enhancing long-term soil health and productivity.
