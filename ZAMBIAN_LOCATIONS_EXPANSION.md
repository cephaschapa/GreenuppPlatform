# Zambian Locations Database Expansion

## 📊 Database Growth

### **Before Expansion:**

- **~50 locations** (mostly Lusaka compounds and major cities)
- Limited rural coverage
- Missing many districts and remote settlements
- Primarily focused on urban areas

### **After Expansion:**

- **~120+ locations**
- Comprehensive district coverage
- Remote villages and settlements
- All 10 provinces well-represented
- Urban, peri-urban, and rural areas
