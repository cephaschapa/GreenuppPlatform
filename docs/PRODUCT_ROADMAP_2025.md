# GreenUpp Platform - Product Roadmap 2025

**Strategic Vision**: Become the leading agricultural technology platform across Sub-Saharan Africa, starting with Zambia

**Mission**: Democratize access to agricultural technology for farmers of all sizes while building sustainable agribusiness ecosystems

---

## 🎯 **Strategic Objectives 2025**

### **Q1 2025: Foundation & Market Validation**

- **Goal**: Establish product-market fit in Zambia
- **Target**: 1,000 paying farmers, 50 agribusiness partners
- **Revenue Target**: $50,000 ARR

### **Q2 2025: Scale & Optimize**

- **Goal**: Achieve profitability in core markets
- **Target**: 5,000 paying farmers, 200 agribusiness partners
- **Revenue Target**: $250,000 ARR

### **Q3 2025: Regional Expansion**

- **Goal**: Enter 2 additional countries (Malawi, Zimbabwe)
- **Target**: 12,000 farmers across 3 countries
- **Revenue Target**: $600,000 ARR

### **Q4 2025: Enterprise & Innovation**

- **Goal**: Launch enterprise solutions and AI features
- **Target**: 20,000 farmers, 500 enterprise clients
- **Revenue Target**: $1,200,000 ARR

## 🔄 **Development Methodology**

### **Agile Framework**

- **Sprint Length**: 2 weeks
- **Release Cycle**: Monthly feature releases
- **Planning Horizon**: Quarterly OKRs with monthly adjustments
- **User Feedback Integration**: Bi-weekly user interviews

### **Quality Assurance**

- **Code Review**: 100% of commits reviewed
- **Testing Coverage**: 90%+ automated test coverage
- **Performance Monitoring**: Real-time metrics and alerts
- **Security Scanning**: Daily automated security scans

### **Risk Management**

- **Technical Risk**: Maintain 6-month technical debt budget
- **Market Risk**: Monthly competitive analysis
- **Operational Risk**: Disaster recovery and backup plans
- **Financial Risk**: Conservative growth assumptions and burn rate monitoring
